# INTRODUCTION

## OPay Transaction Analysis for Q1 and Q2

### Background of the Study

The rapid growth of financial technology (FinTech) has significantly transformed the way individuals and businesses conduct financial transactions, particularly in Nigeria. With the increasing adoption of digital banking, mobile money services, and cashless payment solutions, financial transactions have become faster, more accessible, and more convenient.

Digital payment platforms such as **OPay** have played an important role in this transformation by providing users with access to various financial services, including money transfers, bill payments, airtime purchases, mobile data subscriptions, savings, and other everyday financial activities.

As digital financial services continue to expand, the volume and complexity of financial transactions have increased, creating a growing need for effective transaction monitoring, financial analysis, and data-driven decision-making.

Understanding how money flows into and out of an account, identifying spending patterns, tracking transaction frequency, and evaluating financial activities over time are essential for effective personal financial management and financial planning.

This project, titled **"OPay Transaction Analysis for Q1 and Q2,"** focuses on examining financial transaction records over a six-month period, covering the first and second quarters of the year (January to June).

The analysis aims to provide a comprehensive understanding of financial inflows (credit), financial outflows (debit), transaction frequency, spending behaviour, and the distribution of financial activities across different transaction categories, products, individuals, and time periods.

Using **Microsoft Power BI**, an interactive dashboard was developed to transform raw transaction data into meaningful financial insights through key performance indicators (KPIs), charts, and visual representations.

### Overview of the Dataset

The dataset contains **1,280 financial transactions** recorded across two quarters, representing various credit and debit activities conducted through the OPay platform.

The dashboard provides an overview of the total amount of money received, total amount spent or transferred out, transaction frequency, and financial activities across different months, days, individuals, and transaction categories.

Based on the dashboard, the key financial performance indicators include:

- **Total Credit:** Approximately ₦1,597,725 (₦1.60 million)
- **Total Debit:** Approximately ₦1,609,824 (₦1.61 million)
- **Total Transactions:** 1,280
- **Total Quarters Analyzed:** 2
- **Period Covered:** January to June (Q1 and Q2)
- **Net Transaction Flow:** Approximately -₦12,099

These figures indicate that total debit transactions slightly exceeded total credit transactions during the period under review, resulting in a negative net transaction flow of approximately ₦12,099.

This suggests that financial outflows were marginally higher than inflows during the six-month period. However, this difference does not necessarily indicate a financial loss, as debit transactions may include transfers, savings, purchases, and other financial activities rather than expenses alone.

The dataset also includes information about transaction senders, recipients, transaction categories, financial products, and transaction dates, enabling a detailed examination of financial behaviour and transaction patterns.

### Focus of the Analysis

The analysis explores several important aspects of financial transaction activities.

**1. Credit and Debit Transaction Analysis**

This aspect focuses on evaluating the total amount of money received and the total amount transferred or spent during the period under review.

By comparing credit and debit transactions, the analysis provides insights into financial inflows, outflows, and overall transaction movement.

**2. Monthly Transaction Trends**

The analysis examines how financial transactions vary across the six months, from January to June.

It identifies months with the highest and lowest credit and debit activities, helping to reveal changes in transaction behaviour between the first and second quarters.

**3. Individual Transaction Analysis**

Financial transactions involve different senders and recipients, making it important to understand how money is distributed among individuals.

This analysis examines credit and debit amounts associated with different individuals, identifying those with the highest transaction values and their contributions to overall financial activities.

**4. Transaction Category Analysis**

Financial transactions are classified into different categories, including transfers, deposits, withdrawals, airtime, data, savings, allowances, and other transaction types.

The analysis evaluates how much money is allocated to each category, providing insights into the major sources of financial inflows and outflows.

**5. Financial Product Analysis**

The analysis investigates the distribution of credit and debit transactions across various OPay financial products and transaction purposes.

These include money transfers, deposits, savings, airtime, internet services, food, groceries, and other financial activities represented in the dataset.

This helps identify the products and services that account for the largest share of financial transactions.

**6. Daily Transaction Patterns**

The analysis examines financial activities across the seven days of the week to identify days with higher and lower credit and debit amounts.

Understanding these patterns provides insights into when financial transactions are most active and how transaction behaviour changes throughout the week.

**7. Transaction Frequency and Financial Behaviour**

Beyond the total monetary value of transactions, the analysis also considers transaction frequency to understand the overall level of financial activity.

This provides a broader perspective on transaction behaviour, financial engagement, and the movement of funds over the six-month period.

### Purpose and Significance of the Analysis

The primary purpose of this project is to transform financial transaction records into meaningful and actionable insights that support better financial understanding, monitoring, and decision-making.

By analyzing credit and debit activities, transaction categories, monthly trends, daily patterns, and individual transaction contributions, the project provides a structured view of financial behaviour over time.

The analysis is particularly useful for understanding how funds are received, transferred, saved, and spent across different financial activities.

Furthermore, the project demonstrates the practical application of **business intelligence and data visualization in financial transaction analysis**, highlighting how Microsoft Power BI can be used to simplify complex financial records and present them in a clear, interactive, and easily understandable format.

The findings can support improved financial tracking, spending awareness, budgeting, and financial planning while also demonstrating the value of data analytics in understanding digital payment activities.

# TOOLS USED FOR THE ANALYSIS

## OPay Transaction Analysis for Q1 and Q2

The OPay Transaction Analysis for Q1 and Q2 was carried out using data analytics and business intelligence techniques to examine financial transactions, identify spending patterns, and visualize financial inflows and outflows.

The following tools are relevant to the data preparation, transformation, analysis, and visualization processes.

### 1. Microsoft Excel – Data Cleaning and Preparation

Microsoft Excel can be used for the initial examination, organization, and cleaning of the transaction dataset before importing it into Power BI.

**Key activities include:**

- Identifying and removing duplicate transaction records.
- Checking for missing values and inconsistent entries.
- Standardizing transaction dates, sender names, recipient names, and transaction categories.
- Ensuring credit and debit amounts are correctly formatted as numerical values.
- Organizing transaction records into a structured format for analysis.
- Checking data accuracy and consistency before visualization.

### 2. Microsoft Power BI – Data Analysis and Visualization

Microsoft Power BI was used to develop an interactive dashboard that presents financial transaction activities across the first and second quarters.

**Key activities include:**

- Creating interactive dashboards to visualize credit and debit transactions.
- Analyzing financial inflows and outflows across different months and days.
- Comparing transaction values across individuals, senders, and recipients.
- Visualizing financial activities by transaction categories and products.
- Developing key performance indicators (KPIs) to summarize financial performance.
- Implementing interactive slicers to filter transaction records by individuals, senders, and recipients.
- Comparing financial transaction patterns across Q1 and Q2.

### 3. Power Query – Data Transformation

Power Query, a data transformation tool integrated into Power BI, can be used to clean and prepare raw transaction data for accurate analysis.

**Key activities include:**

- Changing column data types to ensure accurate calculations.
- Handling missing or inconsistent transaction records.
- Renaming columns for clarity and consistency.
- Extracting months, quarters, and weekdays from transaction dates.
- Creating conditional columns to classify financial transactions.
- Preparing the dataset for further analysis and visualization.

### 4. DAX (Data Analysis Expressions) – Calculations and Measures

DAX is a formula language used in Power BI to create calculated measures and perform financial analysis.

It can be used to calculate and summarize important transaction metrics.

**Key calculations include:**

- **Total Credit:** Sum of all incoming transaction amounts.
- **Total Debit:** Sum of all outgoing transaction amounts.
- **Total Transactions:** Count of all recorded financial transactions.
- **Net Transaction Flow:** Total Credit minus Total Debit.
- **Monthly Credit and Debit:** Total transaction values grouped by month.
- **Quarterly Transaction Values:** Credit and debit amounts summarized for Q1 and Q2.

These calculations provide the foundation for evaluating financial performance and identifying transaction patterns.

### 5. Power BI Visualizations – Dashboard Presentation

Different visualization techniques were used to present the financial transaction data clearly and effectively.

**Visualizations include:**

- **KPI Cards:** Display total credit, total debit, total transactions, and number of quarters analyzed.
- **Clustered Column Charts:** Compare monthly credit and debit transactions.
- **Bar Charts:** Identify individuals, senders, and recipients with the highest transaction values.
- **Pie Charts:** Illustrate the distribution of credit and debit transactions across categories and products.
- **Column Charts:** Analyze daily transaction patterns from Sunday to Saturday.
- **Slicers:** Enable interactive filtering by name, sender, and recipient.

These visualizations make it easier to identify trends, compare financial activities, and communicate analytical findings.

### Summary

The combination of **Microsoft Excel, Power Query, DAX, and Microsoft Power BI** provides a structured approach to financial transaction analysis, covering data preparation, transformation, calculation, and visualization.

Through these tools, the OPay transaction dataset can be transformed into an interactive analytical dashboard that supports financial monitoring, spending analysis, and informed decision-making.
